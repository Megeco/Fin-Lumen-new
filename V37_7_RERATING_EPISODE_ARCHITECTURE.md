# v37.7.0 — Dated rerating episode architecture

This release adds the agreed rerating hierarchy as a shadow diagnostic in
Research View. It does not change Swiss Ephemeris, sidereal calculations,
pressure or expansion scores, existing dated windows, current-state language,
Break-Risk rules, or the public green rerating band.

## New research fields

- Current rerating state: Absent, Forming, Active, Contested or Blocked.
- Future rerating outlook: Candidate, Qualified, Durable, Contested or Blocked.
- Projected ignition date.
- Consolidated active rerating window.
- Strongest astro phase, explicitly distinguished from a price peak.
- Contested or blocked passages inside the episode.
- Expansion-sovereignty, leadership-persistence, causal-network and natal-
  authority diagnostics.

## Qualification doctrine

The shadow engine measures the complete dated episode rather than promoting the
first future expansion event. It requires persistent expansion sovereignty,
leadership continuity, macro permission and an operative slow/nodal causal
network. Pure nodal amplification without a slow contact to an operative natal
receptor cannot qualify as rerating. A single exceptionally strong
Jupiter/Saturn/eclipse activation may establish a candidate network.

Research-only natal authority preserves the useful dates but caps the displayed
finding at Future Rerating Candidate. Durable Rerating requires independent
enterprise-chart confirmation. Candidate findings remain outside the public
green band pending validation.

All new outputs are descriptive astrology. They contain no trading, allocation,
position-sizing or price language.
