# v37.6.0 shadow calibration record

## Scope lock

- v37.3.1 remains the authoritative astrology and language layer.
- Existing pressure, expansion, leadership, Break-Risk, window, replay, Swiss
  Ephemeris, and long-cycle outputs are unchanged.
- The new assessment is additive and diagnostic-only.
- It consumes no price, technical, fundamental, or capital-decision input.

## Added diagnostics

- Pressure/expansion trajectory: building, peaking, releasing, or stable.
- Contact trajectory: applying, exact/stationary, or separating, inferred from
  successive Swiss-backed contact orbs.
- Pressure outcome: absorbed pressure, Break-Risk pressure, Break-Risk with
  later repair potential, pressure followed by repair test, or no current
  severe pressure.
- Expansion conversion: support present, contested support, pressure release,
  early expansion, clean expansion, mature expansion, or support decay with
  renewed pressure.
- Episode runway: distinct converted episodes, duration, separation, pressure
  absorption, unresolved serious pressure, and Break-Risk penalties. Repeated
  scan points inside one transit do not create additional episodes.
