## Expansion sequence

1. Support seed
2. Formation / accumulation
3. Ignition watch / activation corridor
4. Rerating active
5. Rerating confirmed
6. Continuation / compressed expansion
7. Acceleration / renewal
8. Expansion exhaustion / support withdrawal

A rerating can form while Pressure is high when support is persistent,
broadening or handing off and the destructive thread is not gaining
sovereignty. A later surge is continuation or acceleration when the original
foundation survived; it is not forced into a second primary-rerating label.

## Pressure sequence

1. Structural warning
2. Vulnerability forming while expansion remains sovereign
3. Pressure activation / sovereignty crossover
4. Structural pressure sovereign
5. Pressure culmination
6. Pressure release

The first appearance of an applying destructive transit is an advance warning,
not the start of an active pressure window. The active window begins near the
sovereignty crossover. Break requires destructive transit/receptor structure,
persistence or handoff and leadership suppression; elapsed time and fall size
cannot create it.

## Recovery sequence

The model distinguishes release-only, support inside unresolved pressure,
failed-recovery risk, recovery formation, genuine recovery and rerating
renewal. A rebound or one favourable transit is not enough.

- Structural Break / continuing destruction
- Expansion exhaustion or support withdrawal plus macro pressure
- Ordinary correction inside an intact expansion foundation
