# v37.5.1 natal additions

## Table comparison

The two user exports contain the same 90 symbols. All 88 resolved symbols have
identical regimes, pressure scores, expansion scores, leadership values, dated
paths, gates, rerating windows, correction modes, and strategic paths. MARUTI
and HBLENGINE LISTING are Natal Pending in both exports. The v37.5.0 table only
separates the earlier combined P/E cell into independent columns and adds score
interpretation labels.

## Added natal records

The following official NSE listing dates are now canonical source-verified
research candidates, calculated at the declared 09:15 Mumbai session proxy:

- MARUTI.NS — 9 Jul 2003
- ACE.NS — 26 Sep 2006
- SCHNEIDER.NS — 20 Mar 2012
- SANSERA.NS — 24 Sep 2021
- NEULANDLAB.NS — 13 Oct 2008
- LAURUSLABS.NS — 19 Dec 2016
- HSCL.NS — 2 Mar 2007
- HBLENGINE.NS — 4 Jan 2007

These dates are source-verified, but chart preference is not yet replay-
validated. Every record therefore retains RESEARCH_ONLY authority and suppresses
proxy angles and houses. Tata Elxsi remains unchanged on its locked,
two-year-validated 8 Feb 1995 listing chart.

HBL ENGINE, HBLENGINE LISTING, and HBLPOWER.NS now resolve to HBLENGINE.NS so a
name change cannot create a second traded-security natal identity.

## Interface

The natal section is now titled **Add or edit natal data**. The stock-add panel
contains a direct link to it and explains the two-step flow: add the ticker,
then save its chart in the editor.
